Privacy Policy

PriceFinder SA does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, password, or other account details.

Location

Location is optional. We request browser location only after you select “Use my location”; it is used for the active search and is not permanently stored. You can instead enter a suburb, city, or postcode.

Anonymous analytics

We may measure aggregate, non-identifying usage such as anonymous search terms and feature performance to improve the service. We do not use mandatory cookies or build profiles from precise location.
